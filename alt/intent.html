Public intent signals

Twenty-five companies whose own repositories are their product, which makes the review bottleneck countable from outside: how long a change waits for its first review, how much of the reviewing two people carry, and what merges with no human review at all.

Reading this list. This is intent measured in a company's own activity rather than inferred. A reviewer already installed is a positive signal: budget and intent exist, and the conversation starts from a comparison. Hover any column heading for what it is, how it was collected, and how to read it.
