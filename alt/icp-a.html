ICP A: velocity accounts

Twenty-five teams shipping far above their headcount, where the review queue is the one thing that did not scale. Every account is confirmed to be building with a named coding agent, then ranked on team shape, distribution and the obligations the code carries.

Reading this list. The fit score ranks engineering team size, distribution across timezones, data sensitivity and staff location. The Why it's here column is the opening line: what the company says about its own review, whether review crosses timezones, and which obligations apply. Hover any column heading for what it is, how it was collected, and how to read it.
v0

Voice companies carry a regulatory boost. EU AI Act transparency obligations took effect on 2 August 2026 and apply directly to voice and speech products, which is a dated, external reason to talk now. The scoring model is an early version, still moving: the weights are a starting point and the inputs are being filled in as collection continues. It is published so the reasoning can be checked.

Where it is absent, the cell stays empty until the figure is collected.HQ?HQThe city the company gives as its headquarters.How: Read from the company's own site and its LinkedIn page.Read it as: Display only. Scoring reads where staff actually sit, since a registered address can sit a long way from the engineers: one account on this list is filed in the Bay Area with over half its people in New York.ICP fit?ICP fitHow well the account matches the kind of company Optimal AI sells to. Structural, changes quarterly, and kept separate from the Intent score, which answers the timing question.How: Five weighted inputs scored 0-100 by tools/icp_score.py: engineering headcount band (50), distributed engineering (22), data sensitivity (16), staff by metro (6), supported platform confirmed (6). Weights sum to 100, so the score reads as a percentage of achievable fit, and two boosts of 10 each sit on top for a customer lookalike and a live regulatory clock.Read it as: An input with no data scores ZERO by decision, so read a low score next to the ceiling: the gap between them is the part still to be collected. Weights are a starting point: halving or doubling any one of them moves the average account only one to six places.Why it's here?Why it's hereThe reason to work this account, strongest and most specific fact first.How: Generated by the build from the facts collected for this company, ordered by how specific each one is. The company's own description of how its code review works leads where the research found one, quoted from its requisitions or its site. After that come a named Optimal customer it competes with, the cities its engineering is split across, engineering headcount as a share of the company, and the compliance regime.Read it as: Every clause traces to something collected for this company, so a row is checkable against its source. Ordering is by specificity, so a shared trait like a compliance badge sits at the back where a distinctive fact exists.
