ICP A: velocity accounts

Twenty-five teams shipping far above their headcount, where the review queue is the one thing that did not scale. Sourced as structural twins of existing customers and ranked on team shape, engineering density and shipping cadence.

Reading this list. The fit score ranks team shape, engineering density, data sensitivity and distribution. The why column is the opening line: how big the engineering team is against the company, whether review crosses timezones, and what obligations the code carries. Hover any column heading for what it is, how it was collected, and how to read it.
v0

Voice companies carry a regulatory boost. EU AI Act transparency obligations took effect on 2 August 2026 and apply directly to voice and speech products, which is a dated, external reason to talk now. The scoring model is an early version, still moving: the weights are a starting point and the inputs are being filled in as collection continues. It is published so the reasoning can be checked.

Company?CompanyCanonical company name, with the sub-line showing what they do or the seed account they were matched from.How: Entities are collapsed by normalized name and bound by DOMAIN, which is the identifier every match is made on. 'Dapta' and 'Dapta (CUSTOMER)' are one row.Read it as: One search for 'Chorus' once returned eleven different companies. If a domain is missing, treat the identity as unconfirmed.What they do?What they doOne line describing the company's product, written from its own site.How: Read from the company's homepage and product pages during research, not from a database category.Read it as: Read it as context. Two companies with the same one-liner can have completely different engineering organizations.Eng?EngEngineering headcount, the dominant input to ICP fit.How: Read from LinkedIn's Engineering function facet. A value shown with a leading < is an upper bound derived from engineering being absent from the top-5 functions, not a measured figure.Read it as: Engineering headcount comes from the people facet alone. Where it is absent, the cell stays empty until the figure is collected.HQ?HQThe city the company gives as its headquarters.How: Read from the company's own site and its LinkedIn page.Read it as: Display only. Scoring reads where staff actually sit, since a registered address can sit a long way from the engineers: one account on this list is filed in the Bay Area with over half its people in New York.ICP fit?ICP fitHow well the account matches the kind of company Optimal AI sells to. Structural, changes quarterly, and kept separate from the Intent score, which answers the timing question.How: Five weighted inputs scored 0-100 by tools/icp_score.py: engineering headcount band (50), distributed engineering (22), data sensitivity (16), staff by metro (6), supported platform confirmed (6). Weights sum to 100, so the score reads as a percentage of achievable fit, and two boosts of 10 each sit on top for a customer lookalike and a live regulatory clock.Read it as: An input with no data scores ZERO by decision, so read a low score next to the ceiling: the gap between them is the part still to be collected. Weights are a starting point: halving or doubling any one of them moves the average account only one to six places.Evidence?EvidenceThe reason to work this account, strongest and most specific fact first.How: Generated by the build from the facts collected for this company, ordered by how specific each one is. The company's own description of how its code review works leads where the research found one, quoted from its requisitions or its site. After that come a named Optimal customer it competes with, the cities its engineering is split across, engineering headcount as a share of the company, and the compliance regime.Read it as: Every clause traces to something collected for this company, so a row is checkable against its source. Ordering is by specificity, so a shared trait like a compliance badge sits at the back where a distinctive fact exists.
