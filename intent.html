Public intent signals

Twenty-five companies whose own repositories are their product, which makes the review bottleneck countable from outside: how long a change waits for its first review, how much of the reviewing two people carry, and how much merges unreviewed.

Reading this list. This is intent measured in a company's own code activity. A reviewer already installed is a positive signal: budget and intent exist, and the conversation starts from a comparison. Hover any column heading for what it is, how it was collected, and how to read it.
