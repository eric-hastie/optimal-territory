ICP B: governance accounts

Twenty-five regulated organizations where agent output is supervised and verified before it ships, and the record of what was reviewed carries as much weight as the review. Built from public registries that carry a dated, checkable obligation.

Reading this list. Compliance is what the company states about itself on its own site, with the sentence kept as evidence. A trust portal is public proof that a vendor-review gate exists, which tells you the shape of the cycle before the first call. Hover any column heading for what it is, how it was collected, and how to read it.
v0

Governance fit is scored on published posture. Compliance, procurement surface, scale and the strength of the sourcing route. The scoring model is an early version, still moving: the weights are a starting point and the inputs are being filled in as collection continues. It is published so the reasoning can be checked.
