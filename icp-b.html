ICP B: governance accounts

Twenty-five regulated organisations where agent output is supervised and verified before it ships, and the record of what was reviewed carries as much weight as the review. Built from public registries that carry a dated, checkable obligation.

Reading this list. Compliance is what the company states about itself on its own site, with the sentence kept as evidence. A trust portal is public proof that a vendor-review gate exists, which tells you the shape of the cycle before the first call.
