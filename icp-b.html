ICP B: regulated organizations

Twenty-five regulated organizations where agent output is supervised and verified before it ships, and review has to leave a record an auditor can read. Built from public registries that carry a dated, checkable obligation.

Reading this list. Compliance is what the company states about itself on its own site, with the sentence kept as evidence. A trust portal is public proof that a vendor-review gate exists, which tells you the shape of the cycle before the first call. Hover any column heading for what it is, how it was collected, and how to read it.
v0

Governance fit is scored on published posture. Compliance 40, agent adoption 20, procurement surface 20, scale 15, sourcing route strength 5. The scoring model is an early version, still moving: the weights are a starting point and the inputs are being filled in as collection continues. It is published so the reasoning can be checked.

Company?CompanyCanonical company name, with the sub-line showing what they do or the seed account they were matched from.How: Entities are collapsed by normalized name and bound by DOMAIN, which is the identifier every match is made on. If a domain is missing, treat the identity as unconfirmed.What they do?What they doOne line describing the company's product, written from its own site.How: Read from the company's homepage and product pages during research, not from a database category.Read it as: Read it as context. Two companies with the same one-liner can have completely different engineering organizations.Sourcing route?Sourcing routeThe public source this account was found through.How: Compliance certification registries, healthcare and lending regulatory footprints, insurance and government platform categories, PCI scope, and peers of published customers.Read it as: A route explains how an account was found. Registry-sourced rows carry a dated obligation; category-sourced rows carry a shape.Compliance?ComplianceRegimes the company states it holds, in its own words on its own site.How: Read from trust, security and compliance pages. Claims the company makes about itself are what count here; a hosting provider's certificate is recorded separately as the provider's.Read it as: An in-progress program is shown as such. That is often the better moment to arrive, because the work is funded and underway.Agents?AgentsThe coding agents this company names in its own material.How: Read from the company's own requisitions and its site, matched on the tool by name: Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Copilot, Windsurf, Devin, OpenClaw. Where the material describes an agent-led product, the cell says so.Read it as: This says agent-written code is arriving at the review queue, and nothing about how much. On the ICP A list it is the entry filter and scores nothing, since selecting on a signal and then scoring it flattens a board. On the ICP B list it is scored, at 20 of 100, because both enterprise customers bought Insights for reporting and audit. An empty cell means no tool was named in the material collected, not that none is in use.Trust portal?Trust portalWhether the company runs a public trust or security portal.How: Detected from the company's own pages and the portal vendors behind them.Read it as: This is the clearest public evidence that a vendor-review gate exists, which tells you the shape of the sales cycle before the first call.Fit score?Fit scoreGovernance fit: how strongly an organization carries obligations that make verified, evidenced review a requirement.How: Five weighted inputs: compliance posture 40, agent adoption 20, procurement surface 20, scale 15, sourcing route strength 5.Read it as: Provisional. The model is built on what is measurable from public sources today, and it gains inputs as security hiring and published AI policies are collected.
