ICP A: velocity accounts

Twenty-five teams shipping far above their headcount, where the review queue is the one thing that did not scale. Sourced as structural twins of existing customers and ranked on team shape, engineering density and shipping cadence.

Reading this list. The fit score ranks team shape, engineering density, data sensitivity and distribution. The why column is the opening line: how big the engineering team is against the company, whether review crosses timezones, and what obligations the code carries. Hover any column heading for what it is, how it was collected, and how to read it.
v0

Voice companies carry a regulatory boost. EU AI Act transparency obligations took effect on 2 August 2026 and apply directly to voice and speech products, which is a dated, external reason to talk now. The scoring model is an early version, still moving: the weights are a starting point and the inputs are being filled in as collection continues. It is published so the reasoning can be checked.

Two companies with the same one-liner can have completely different engineering organizations.Eng?EngEngineering headcount, the dominant input to ICP fit.How: Read from LinkedIn's Engineering function facet. A value shown with a leading < is an upper bound derived from engineering being absent from the top-5 functions, not a measured figure.Read it as: Engineering headcount comes from the people facet alone. Where it is absent, the cell stays empty until the figure is collected.Eng share?Eng shareEngineering as a percentage of total staff.How: Engineering headcount divided by total staff, both from the LinkedIn people facet.Read it as: Fifteen engineers in a 236-person company is a different organization from fifteen in a thirty-person company. This column is what tells those apart.HQ?HQThe city the company gives as its headquarters.How: Read from the company's own site and its LinkedIn page.Read it as: Display only. Scoring reads where staff actually sit, since a registered address can sit a long way from the engineers: one account on this list is filed in the Bay Area with over half its people in New York.ICP fit?ICP fitHow well the account matches the kind of company Optimal AI sells to. Structural, changes quarterly, and kept separate from the Intent score, which answers the timing question.How: Six weighted terms scored 0-100 by tools/icp_score.py: engineering headcount band (42), SCM present (18), engineering share of staff (15), data sensitivity (10), distributed engineering (8), staff by metro (7). Weights sum to 100, so the score reads as a percentage of achievable fit.Read it as: A term with no data scores ZERO by decision, so read a low score next to the ceiling: the gap between them is the part still to be collected. Weights are a starting point: halving or doubling any one of them moves the average account only one to six places.Evidence?EvidenceThe reason each term scored what it scored, term by term. Hover the cell for the full text.How: Written by the scorer as it runs, quoting the source of each value: which regime was named and whether the company claimed it itself, which GitHub org was matched and how it was bound to the domain, whether staff locations were read.Read it as: This is where a score stops being a number and becomes checkable.
