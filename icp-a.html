Twenty-five teams shipping far above their headcount, where the review queue is the one thing that did not scale. Sourced as structural twins of existing customers and ranked on team shape, engineering density and shipping cadence.

Reading this list. The fit score ranks team shape, engineering density, data sensitivity and distribution. Metro is scored, never a gate.ICP fit?ICP fitHow well the account matches the kind of company Optimal AI sells to. Structural, changes quarterly, and NEVER summed with the Intent score.How: Six weighted terms scored 0-100 by tools/icp_score.py: engineering headcount band (42), SCM present (18), engineering share of staff (15), data sensitivity (10), distributed engineering (8), staff by metro (7). Weights sum to 100, so the score reads as a percentage of achievable fit.Read it as: A term with no data scores ZERO by decision, so a low score can mean 'unmeasured' rather than 'bad fit'.
