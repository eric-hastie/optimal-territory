ICP A: velocity accounts

Twenty-five teams shipping far above their headcount, where the review queue is the one thing that did not scale. Sourced as structural twins of existing customers and ranked on team shape, engineering density and shipping cadence.

Reading this list. Every score sits next to a ceiling and a coverage marker. The ceiling is what the account would score if the unmeasured terms came back full, so the gap tells you how much of a row is measured. The evidence column carries the reason each term scored what it did. Hover any column heading for what it is, how it was collected, and how to read it.

Metro is scored, never a gate.ICP fit?ICP fitHow well the account matches the kind of company Optimal AI sells to. Structural, changes quarterly, and NEVER summed with the Intent score.How: Six weighted terms scored 0-100 by tools/icp_score.py: engineering headcount band (42), SCM present (18), engineering share of staff (15), data sensitivity (10), distributed engineering (8), staff by metro (7). Weights sum to 100, so the score reads as a percentage of achievable fit.Read it as: A term with no data scores ZERO by decision, so a low score can mean 'unmeasured' rather than 'bad fit'. Always read it next to the ceiling. Weights are a starting point: halving or doubling any one of them moves the average account only one to six places.ICP ceiling?ICP ceilingThe ICP score this account would reach if every unmeasured term came back full.How: Recomputed alongside the score, substituting 100 for each term that has no data.Read it as: This is the honest half of the missing-data policy. A score of 30 against a ceiling of 34 is a bad account; a score of 30 against a ceiling of 88 is an account nobody has measured. The gap is the part of the rating that is guesswork, not judgment.Terms measured?Terms measuredHow many of the six ICP terms have real data behind them.How: Counted per row by tools/icp_score.py. A term counts as measured when it was either found or actively looked for and absent.Read it as: Absent is not the same as unmeasured. Staff locations read with no offshore cluster is a measurement; no location record at all is a gap. Only the second is enrichment work.Evidence?EvidenceThe reason each term scored what it scored, term by term. Hover the cell for the full text.How: Written by the scorer as it runs, quoting the source of each value: which regime was named and whether the company claimed it itself, which GitHub org was matched and how it was bound to the domain, whether staff locations were read.Read it as: This is where a score stops being a number and becomes checkable.
