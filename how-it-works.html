Fit scoring

How both lists are built

One filter decides who gets looked at. Two models with unique scoring methods decide how they rank, and both are built from what the published customers have in common.

The filter comes first

Applied before any account is scored.

Every company on these lists is already confirmed to be building or shipping with coding agents, evidenced in their own requisitions or on their own site: Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Copilot and Windsurf by name, or a product that is itself agent-led. This filter is applied before accounts are added to the universe, so it does not exist in the scoring model.

The review bottleneck Optimal removes arrives directly behind agent adoption, so the list starts where the problem already exists.

The ICP A score

Five weighted inputs summing to 100, so a score reads as a percentage of achievable fit.

Engineering headcount band 50, distributed engineering 22, data sensitivity 16, staff in the event metros 6, supported platform confirmed 6. Two boosts sit on top and are recorded separately: +10 where an account is a competitor or structural twin of a named customer, since the reference already exists, and +10 where a live regulatory clock applies, currently the EU AI Act transparency duties on voice products.

The ICP B score

Five weighted inputs summing to 100: compliance posture 40, agent adoption 20, procurement surface 20, scale 15, and sourcing route strength 5. A registry entry with a date on it counts for more than a category match, since it gives the outreach a reason to happen this quarter.

The two models share no inputs, and compliance moves the other way, from a 16-point input in the velocity model to the largest single input here, because it is what defines the segment.
