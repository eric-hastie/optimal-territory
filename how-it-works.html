The score

Six terms summing to 100, so a score reads as a percentage of achievable fit.

Engineering headcount band 50, data sensitivity 18, distributed engineering 9, engineering share 8, staff in the event metros 8, supported platform confirmed 7. Two boosts sit on top and are recorded separately: +10 where an account is a competitor or structural twin of a named customer, since the reference already exists, and +10 where a live regulatory clock applies, currently the EU AI Act transparency duties on voice products.
